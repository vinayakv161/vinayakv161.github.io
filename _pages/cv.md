Research Profile
=========
Biophysicist studying how chromatin encodes and loses memory of prior states. I combine super-resolution imaging and epigenomic sequencing with polymer modeling and machine learning to determine why some perturbation-induced chromatin states persist after the stimulus is removed while others revert.

Research Experience
===============
* **Postdoctoral Researcher**, University of Pennsylvania, Shenoy Lab (Jul 2026 – Present)
  * Completing studies of chromatin memory and mechano-senescence and preparing both for submission.
* **PhD Researcher**, University of Pennsylvania, Shenoy Lab (2021 – Jul 2026)
  * _Determinants of epigenetic memory:_ Established that whether a perturbed chromatin region reverts or retains its induced state is set by its local domain architecture, with boundary-proximal regions accounting for most reversion and most retention. Identified impaired rebuilding of H3K9me3-marked heterochromatin as the basis of an asymmetric, history-dependent memory that progresses toward senescence under chronic stimulation.
  * _Perturbation and multi-omic readout:_ Applied pharmacological (HDAC and EZH2 inhibition) and mechanical perturbations followed by washout, and read out the response with STORM/OligoSTORM, Oligopaint/FISH, confocal imaging, Hi-C, ATAC-seq, ChIP-seq, and bulk and single-cell RNA-seq to connect nanoscale architecture to epigenetic state and transcription.
  * _Predictive modeling:_ Built sequencing-informed polymer models in which domain organization emerges from competition between diffusion of epigenetic marks and writer/eraser reactions, yielding testable scaling predictions confirmed by imaging and sequencing. Developed physics-informed machine-learning methods to infer spatially varying reaction rates directly from super-resolution images, and extended these to multimodal prediction across conformation, accessibility and transcription.
* **Long-Term Visiting Student**, International Centre for Theoretical Sciences, Bangalore (2020 – 2021)
  * Developed applied mathematical methods and convolutional neural-network models to predict Indian monsoon patterns from sea-surface temperature and large-scale climatic signals.
* **Undergraduate Senior Thesis**, Jawaharlal Nehru Centre for Advanced Scientific Research, Bangalore (2019 – 2020)
  * Developed machine-learning models for DNA conductance using atomic and graph-based representations, achieving roughly 80× speedup over density functional theory calculations.
* **Undergraduate Researcher**, University of Manchester & NCRA (2018 – 2019)
  * Developed real-time streaming and computational methods for Square Kilometre Array data analysis and pulsar dynamics.
